The Nether

Once you are done with the resources, you have to fill your bucket with water, and try finding a lavapool (they can spawn in surfaces or deep in caves, but its recommended to try and find them on surfaces). Once that is done, pour the bucket a block above the lavapool and it should turn into obsidian. Once that is done, mine 8-10 obsidian with your diamond pickaxe and build a portal that looks like this the image above and light it.


When in the nether, start running until you find a nether fortress, it might take a while so it is recommended to stock up on food and blocks (to bridge across nether islands) but you will eventually find one. Another method is using a nether fortress finder site such as this one, you just need to enter the seed in.

Chunkbase Nether Fortress Finder

When you are in the fortress, kill blazes (gold creatures) and collect 10 blaze rods. When in the fortress, be mindful of wither skeletons and ghasts as they will also try and kill you. When done, exit the fortress and mine for gold, and use that gold to trade with the piglins. They have a chance of dropping ender pearls, which is the 2nd ingrident needed for ender eyes. Obtain at least 20. While you’re there, obtain at least a stack or two of arrows.
