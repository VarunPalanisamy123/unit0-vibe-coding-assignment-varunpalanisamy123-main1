Resources:

When you create a new world, start by chopping up trees, finding food, and getting basic stone tools. Then, you can enter caves and try and find iron (ideally y16). You need this iron for adequite armor and tools, or you will die easily in the later stages. You should also get 3 diamonds to craft a diamond pickaxe in order to mine obsidian for the nether. Diamonds can spawn anywhere below deepslate, so it is good to look in deepslate caves.

Some other essential things to obtain are a bucket (3 iron), flint and steel (1 iron, 1 flint from gravel) and haybales (9 wheat) to reduce fall damage. Also get 4 gold to craft gold boots so the piglins in the nether don’t attack you (They attack non gold armored people). Make sure to also get a bow.
