Finding the Stronghold:

Throw Eyes of Ender and follow the direction they travel. Continue throwing them until you locate the Stronghold. Because Eyes of Ender can break when used, bringing at least 20 is recommended.

Once the Eye begins moving downward, dig carefully until you discover the Stronghold. Explore its hallways and rooms until you locate the End Portal room.

Place Eyes of Ender into every empty portal frame and prepare for the final battle.
