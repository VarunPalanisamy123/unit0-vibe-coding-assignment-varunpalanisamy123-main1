Resources:

When you create a new world, beginon ore. Iron armor and tools are essential for surviving the later stages of the game.

You should also obtain at least three diamonds to craft a diamond pickaxe. This allows you to mine obsidian, which will be needed to reach the Nether. Diamonds can generate below Y-level 0, making deep caves one of the best places to search.

Other important resources include a water bucket, flint and steel, hay bales to reduce fall damage, a bow, arrows, and enough gold to craft gold boots. Piglins in the Nether will not attack players wearing at least one piece of gold armor.
